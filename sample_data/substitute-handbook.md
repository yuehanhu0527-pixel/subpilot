# Lincoln Elementary — Substitute Handbook

## Welcome

Thank you for covering for us today. This handbook covers the essentials:
attendance, the bathroom pass policy, lunch procedures, and emergencies.

## Bathroom Pass Policy

Students must use the hall pass lanyard when leaving the classroom. Only
one student may be out at a time. Bathroom passes are limited to 5 minutes
unless a note from the office or the nurse says otherwise. Escort younger
students (K–2) to the nearest restroom.

## Attendance

Take attendance in the first 10 minutes of each period and send the slip
to the office with a student helper. If a student is absent, write their
name on the blue slip — do not mark attendance in the gradebook.

## Fire Drill

When the fire alarm sounds during a fire drill, lead students out the
nearest exterior door to the field in single file. Take the red emergency
folder and take attendance once on the field. During the drill, students
must stay silent and walk — never run. Do not re-enter the building until
the principal gives the all-clear.

## Lunch Procedures

Walk students to the cafeteria at the start of the lunch period. Students
with allergies sit at the nut-free table on the left. Pick up your class
from the cafeteria doors.

## Office & Emergency Contacts

The front office extension is 100. For behavior emergencies, call the
principal at extension 101. The nurse is at extension 102.
